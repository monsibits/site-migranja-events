FAQs

A few things people often ask.

If your question is not here — about a date, a layout, or something particular to your celebration — write to us, or book a visit and ask it on the grounds.

Where is Mi Granja?

Balagbag, Mahabang Kahoy Lejos, Indang, Cavite, Region 4-A (CALABARZON), 4122. Open the address in Google Maps.

How do I book an ocular visit or send an inquiry?

Use the contact form to book an ocular visit, request a quote, or send a general inquiry, or email sales@migranjaevents.com. You can share a preferred date for a visit.

Which venues can we use?

The grounds include the Ballroom, The Nook, and Amore Garden. A celebration may use one space or more than one; we can walk the options with you on an ocular visit.

Do you host weddings?

Yes. Mi Granja is a wedding venue in Indang, Cavite, and a setting for other special celebrations for couples, families, and organizations.

How are rates set?

Rates depend on the date, guest count, and venue. We do not list package prices on this site. Request a quote or book an ocular visit, and we will follow up with a quotation.

Are there rooms on the property?

Yes. The grounds include a bridal suite and Rooms 103 and 104. Details, availability, and rates for a stay are shared when you inquire.

What is the story of the place?

Mi Granja began as a humble family farm in Indang, Cavite, and grew into a venue shaped around slow luxury and grounded living. You can read the fuller story on the About page.

Still have a question?

We are glad to answer it in writing, or on a walk through the grounds.

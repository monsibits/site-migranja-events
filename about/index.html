Our Story

Nestled in the serene corners of Indang, Cavite, Mi Granja began as a humble family farm — a place rooted in land, legacy, and the love of gathering people together.

Over the years, it evolved into a thoughtfully curated space where every corner tells a story, every pathway invites reflection, and every detail is designed to connect guests to nature, heritage, and each other.

Mi Granja is more than a venue; it is the art of Grounded Living in motion. Here, life slows down, allowing guests to savor each moment, feel fully present, and reconnect with what truly matters. Nature is not just scenery — it is a companion, shaping experiences with sunlight, greenery, and the quiet rhythm of the land.

Every design choice, from architecture inspired by global nature aesthetics to interiors infused with warmth and texture, reflects Slow Luxury — premium comfort delivered at an unhurried pace, where elegance meets intimacy, and thoughtful details transform ordinary moments into lasting memories.

Mi Granja invites its guests to step away from the rush of everyday life, immerse themselves in a space that honors both legacy and nature, and experience gatherings that are meaningful, intentional, and unforgettable.

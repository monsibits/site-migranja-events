The grounds

A family farm, opened for celebration.

Nestled in the quieter corners of Indang, Cavite, Mi Granja began as a humble family farm — a place rooted in land, legacy, and the love of gathering people together.

Today it is a thoughtfully kept setting for weddings and special celebrations, where open air, planted paths, and unhurried rooms invite you to slow down and stay a while.

The Nook.

A garden building set among the planted paths — wooden doors, a latticed window, and a quieter scale than the main pavilion.

It lends itself to more intimate gatherings. As with every space on the grounds, the right use of The Nook depends on your celebration; we will walk it with you on an ocular visit.
